# PhotonShield
 
**Photon Shield assembly for no-noise visual stimulation during two-photon imaging in mice**

This repository contains the 3D CAD models of the **Photon Shield**, a modular optical shielding system designed for two-photon calcium imaging experiments combined with visual stimulation in mice. The system minimizes stray-light contamination while preserving experimental flexibility and fast handling.

Detailed experimental validation, benchmarking, and use cases are described in the accompanying publication.

## Repository contents

The repository includes CAD models for:

- Headplate
- Two-part cylindrical objective shield
- Objective-mounted top shielding (“Sombrero”)
- Home-cage protection cap
- Custom holder rod and clamp


## Headholder for shield and probe wide v4mod

![Headholder for shield and probe wide v4mod](./Headholder%20for%20shield%20and%20probe%20wide%20v4mod.jpg)

This is the headplate with rails for mounting „Shield Back 10XSVMP v4” and „headholder cap derived v4”. Bottom has opening for a 5 mm coverslip. 

**Important:**  
Care must be taken not to put glue or cement to the top edge of the headplate.



## Shield Back 10XSVMP v4

![Shield Back 10XSVMP v4](./Shield%20Back%2010XSVMP%20v4.jpg)

This is one side of the shield cylinder that should be mounted first onto the headplate. It is typically mounted from the back of the animal. After mounting, immersion gel can be applied generously.

## Shield Front 10XSVMP derived v4

![Shield Front 10XSVMP derived v4](./Shield%20Front%2010XSVMP%20derived%20v4.jpg)

This is the complementary part of the shield cylinder. We attach a ~20 cm long piece of black insulating tape to its outer surface, allowing it to overhang on both sides. After precise insertion, the overhanging parts are smoothed over from both sides to adhere to the other half of the shield. During the process, the microscope’s green illumination is used to make any misalignment visible.


## Headholder cap derived v4

![Headholder cap derived v4](./headholder%20cap%20derived%20v4.jpg)

This part is fitted onto the headplate when the animal is returned to its home cage.

## Sombrero 10XSVMP v3

![Sombrero 10XSVMP v3](./Sombrero%2010XSVMP%20v3.jpg)

This is the top shielding component. It must be slid onto the objective from the rear before operation.

## Sombrero XLUMPlanFLN 20x

![Sombrero XLUMPFLN 20x](./Sombrero%20XLUMPFLN%2020x.jpg)

This variant of the top shield fits both:

- **Olympus XLUMPlanFLN 20×**
- **Nikon CFI75 LWD16× W**

As with the other Sombrero design, it must be slid onto the objective from the rear prior to imaging.


## Holder rod (wide v3)

![Holder rod wide v3](./holder%20rod%20wide%20v3.jpg)

This is the clamp used to hold the headplate. It was custom machined from stainless steel. For anesthetized applications, it can also be 3D printed (select the appropriate configuration inside the model).

![Holder clamp wide v2](./holder%20clamp%20wide%20v2.jpg)
